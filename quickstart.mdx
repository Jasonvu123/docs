---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from a fresh browser to a settled first trade on updown."
---

This guide takes you from signing in to watching your first window settle.

<Warning>
Trading on updown uses **real money**. The amount you spend on contracts is at risk.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    On the updown landing page, click **Start Trading**. You land on the **Live** hub. Click **Login** in the top right and sign in with your **email** or **Google** account. updown uses [Privy](https://privy.io) for authentication, so there is no browser extension or seed phrase. An embedded wallet is created for you automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tell us about you (first time only)">
    On the **Tell us about you** form, enter your **Name** (required) and optional **Phone**, answer the four quick dropdowns about your trading background, and click **Submit**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Allow trading">
    Click the **Portfolio / Cash** chip in the top bar to open the **Wallet** modal. The first time, it shows the **Enable trading** panel. Click **Allow trading**, wait for "Trading enabled", then click **Done**.

    This one-time approval lets updown convert your deposits and place your trades instantly. See [Account setup](/account-setup) for what it covers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit USDC on Polygon">
    In the **Wallet** modal under **Deposit**, click **Copy Address** (or scan the QR code) and send at least **$2 of USDC on the Polygon network** to that address from your exchange or wallet.

    Keep the modal open, or come back to it later. It tracks the conversion live: *Deposit detected… → Finalizing… → Converting your deposit to pUSD…* and finishes with the toast "Deposit converted to pUSD". Click **Go to Portfolio** to confirm your **Available** balance.

    <Warning>
    Send **USDC on Polygon only**. Wrong tokens or networks may result in permanent loss. Deposits under $2 sit unconverted until you top them up.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a market">
    Click **Live** in the top bar and choose a card, for example "Bitcoin (BTC) · 15 min". The market page shows **To beat** (the price when the window opened), **Now** (the live price), and **Time left**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hold to buy">
    In the ticket (the right rail on desktop, the bottom sheet on a phone) stay on **Quick Buy**. Press and hold the **$1** box under **Up** or **Down** until it fills. A **Trade executed** card confirms the fill, for example "Bought $1.00 Up @ 51¢ · Pays $1.96 if Up wins".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch it settle">
    Watch **Time left** count down. When the window closes, your position reads "settling". Within about 5 minutes, **Portfolio → history** shows **Won** or **Lost**, and your **Cash** updates. There is nothing to claim.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Optional: sell back early">
    Changed your mind before the close? Hold **Sell** on your row in the **Live Positions** panel on the market page. Your realized profit or loss is settled immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A $1 Quick Buy on a thin quote can fill partially. The confirmation reads "Filled $X of your $Y — book was thin here". If the price moves sharply between the quote and your fill, updown rejects the order rather than filling at a much worse price. See [Placing orders](/trading/placing-orders).
</Note>
