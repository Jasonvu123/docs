updown is where you **trade movement on everything**. Every market tracks one live number — a stock price, a crypto price, a team's live win odds, or a prediction-market outcome's chance — and cuts it into **windows**. When a window opens, it stamps the number at that instant as the level **to beat**. You buy **Up** or **Down** contracts against updown's live quote, priced from 1¢ to 99¢. When the window closes, every contract on the winning side pays **$1.00** and the other side pays $0.

The most you can lose is what you paid. You can sell back at any time before the window closes, and winners are paid automatically when it settles.

<Warning>
updown is **real-money trading**. Contracts are funded by USDC you deposit, and losses are real. What you pay for your contracts is the most you can lose on them.
</Warning>

## What you can do

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign in with your **email** or **Google** account. An embedded wallet is created for you — no browser extension, no seed phrase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow trading once">
    A one-time **Allow trading** approval lets updown convert your deposits and place your trades without a signature for every action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit USDC on Polygon">
    Send at least **$2 of USDC on Polygon** to your deposit address. It converts to **pUSD**, your trading balance, automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a side">
    Open **Live** or **Trade**, choose a market, and hold a **$1**, **$5**, or **$10** box under **Up** or **Down**. Your order fills instantly at the live quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it settle">
    When the window closes, winning contracts pay $1.00 each and your cash updates automatically. There is no claim step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or sell back early">
    Hold **Sell** on your row in **Live Positions** to close a position before the window ends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Market** | One tracked number, such as "Apple (AAPL)", "Bitcoin (BTC)", or a live game's win odds, that runs a series of windows. |
| **Window** | One round of a market. It opens on a fixed grid, stamps the opening value, and closes after its cadence: 15 minutes, a day, a week, or a month. |
| **To beat** | The market's value at the instant the window opened. Up wins only if the close is strictly above it. |
| **Up / Down** | The two sides of a window. Each contract pays $1.00 if its side wins and $0 if it loses. A window that closes exactly unchanged settles **Down**. |
| **Contract** | The unit you trade, priced 1¢–99¢. The price is the market's implied chance that the side wins. "Pays $5.00 if Up" means you hold 5 Up contracts. |
| **Quick Buy** | Hold-to-buy boxes for $1, $5, and $10. Press and hold to fill at the live quote. |
| **Live vs. Trade** | **Live** runs 15-minute windows on stocks, crypto, and in-play sports. **Trade** runs daily, weekly, and monthly windows on stocks, crypto, and prediction-market outcomes. |
| **Cash vs. Portfolio** | **Cash** is pUSD that is free to trade or withdraw. **Portfolio** is cash plus the live value of your open positions. |
| **pUSD** | Your trading balance. USDC deposited on Polygon converts to pUSD automatically, and withdrawals convert back to native USDC. |
| **Settlement** | Automatic. When a window closes, winners are paid $1 per contract and losers' cost is collected, usually within about 5 minutes. |

## Two hubs, one mechanic

Both hubs use the same ticket, the same rules, and the same settlement. They differ only in how long a window lasts and which markets they list.

- **Live** — 15-minute windows. Stocks trade weekdays 4:00 AM – 8:00 PM ET, crypto trades 24/7, and sports markets run from kickoff to the final whistle.
- **Trade** — one window per day, week, or month on stocks, crypto, and prediction-market outcomes. Windows follow UTC boundaries.

<Note>
There is no trading fee on updown today. The only cost is the spread between updown's bid and ask, which is at least 2¢. See [Placing orders](/trading/placing-orders) for how quotes work.
</Note>
