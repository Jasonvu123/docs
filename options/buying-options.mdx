Options fill instantly at the live quote. There are two ways to place an order: the **order panel** on a stock page (desktop and tablet), and the **quick-buy popup** (Home, and the docked bar on phones).

## The order panel

Select a contract from Quick trade or the chain and the panel fills in:

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Title** | The trade and date, for example "Above $770 · Sep 24". "No trade selected" until you pick one. The first **Up** trade is preselected. |
| **Outlook** / **Instrument** | The outlook chip and the leg, for example "Buy $770 Call · Sep 24". Two-leg trades list both legs. |
| **Amount** | Toggle **Contracts** (default, starts at 1) or **Dollars**. The other unit shows alongside ("= $1.23" or "= 4.07 contracts"). Dollars convert to contracts at the ask, rounded to 0.01. |
| **Readouts** | **Cost**, **Max loss**, **Max profit** ("Uncapped" for calls and two-sided trades), **Breakeven** or **Breakevens**, **% chance ITM**, and **Wallet** "$X USDG". |
| **Payoff** | A chart of profit and loss at the close, with a strip showing **Max loss**, **Break even**, and **Max profit**. Drag the chart to read "Profit at $X". |
| **Button** | **Buy $4.92**. It reads **Sign in to trade** when signed out, **Deposit to buy** when the cost exceeds your wallet, **Placing…** while it fills, or **Coming soon** for Neutral. |

On success the button turns green with **Bought for $4.92**, then the take box appears (see [Profiles & social](/platform/profiles-and-social)). On failure the server's message shows in red.

## The quick-buy popup

Opened from Home's option cards and from the phone bar. It always uses the **nearest expiry**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a direction">
    Tabs **Up**, **Down**, or **Up or Down** (a call and a put together).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a row">
    Three rows such as "Above $770.00" with a distance tag ("▲ $4.57") and the per-contract price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an amount">
    Chips **$1 / $5 / $10** (opens on $5) or type an amount. Contracts are the amount divided by the price, rounded down to 0.01, so you are never charged more than you entered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Read the summary line, for example "Max loss $4.92 · profit above $771.23", and tap **▲ Buy above $770.00 · $4.92**. The footer reads **"Bought for $4.92 — settles at the close"**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Other footer lines you may see: "Enter at least $1.00", "Closed for the last 15 minutes of the session", "Not quoting right now", "Couldn't load prices — try again in a moment", or your **Wallet** balance.

## Limits and rules

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| **Maximum per buy** | **$100 of premium.** Over that: "One buy is capped at $100 for now (this one is $X)." |
| **Minimum** | 0.01 contracts and $0.01 of cost. The popup requires $1. |
| **Legs** | Up to two, each a buy: a single call or put, or a call and put together. |
| **Order type** | Market only, at the current ask. No limit or stop orders. |
| **Buying power** | The USDG in your wallet. The premium leaves your wallet immediately. |
| **Price protection** | The server re-quotes at the moment of the buy. If the cost rose more than 2% since you saw it, the buy is refused: "Price moved: $X now, you saw $Y." A lower price fills at the lower price. |
| **Stale prices** | No quote is offered if the on-chain price is more than 15 seconds old. |
| **Partial fills** | None. A buy is all or nothing, and a failed buy takes nothing. |
| **Fees** | None. |

## Errors you may see

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| "Sign in first." | You are signed out. |
| "No wallet on this account yet." / "Account not set up yet — sign in again." | Account setup did not finish. Sign out and in again. |
| "This account can't trade." | The account is restricted. See [Support](/help/support#restricted-accounts). |
| "That expiry is no longer offered." / "Too close to the close to trade." | You are inside the last 15 minutes before that expiry. |
| "Not quoting right now." / "No quote for the $770 call." | No price is available for that contract at the moment. |
| "Price moved: $X now, you saw $Y." | Price protection refused the buy. Try again at the new price. |
| "Too small." | Below the minimum. |
| "One buy is capped at $100 for now (this one is $X)." | Reduce the amount. |
| "Couldn't take $X USDG from your wallet: …" | The wallet transfer failed, for example because trading is not enabled. See [Account & profile](/account-and-profile#allow-trading). |
