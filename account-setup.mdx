## Sign in

updown uses [Privy](https://privy.io) for authentication. Click **Login** in the top right and sign in with your **email address** or a **Google account**. These are the only two login methods.

When you sign in for the first time, Privy creates an **embedded wallet** for you automatically. The wallet is tied to your login, so there is no seed phrase to manage and no browser extension to install.

<Info>
If you opened a specific market before signing in, you return to it after login.
</Info>

## Onboarding

After your first sign-in, a short **Tell us about you** form asks for:

- **Name** (required)
- **Phone** (optional)
- **What kind of trader are you?**
- **Where do you trade today?**
- **Experience level**
- **How'd you hear about us?**

Click **Submit** to finish. If you arrived through a friend's referral link, the referral is attributed to your account at this step.

## Allow trading

Before your first deposit can convert, you complete a one-time approval. Open the **Wallet** modal (click the **Portfolio / Cash** chip in the top bar, or **Deposit** on the Portfolio page). The first time, it shows the **Enable trading** panel. Click **Allow trading**.

The panel reads "Approving trading access…" while it works. You can close the modal, and the approval keeps going in the background. When it finishes, the panel reads "Trading enabled" and "You're all set — deposit funds and start trading." Click **Done**.

This approval lets updown convert your deposits to pUSD and place your trades instantly, without asking you to sign every transaction. Your keys stay in your Privy embedded wallet.

## Your wallets

Your account uses one user-facing chain, **Polygon**, with two addresses. Both are listed in the **Wallet** card on your Profile page, each with a copy button and a "View on Polygonscan" link:

| Address | What it is |
|---------|-----------|
| **Signing wallet (Polygon)** | Your Privy embedded wallet. This is the address you **send USDC to**. It is also the address shown in the Wallet modal's deposit card. |
| **Deposit wallet (Polygon)** | The wallet that **holds your pUSD** trading balance. |

See [Deposits & withdrawals](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw) for the funding flow.

## Your balance

Your trading balance is **pUSD**. USDC you deposit converts to pUSD automatically, and withdrawals convert back to native USDC. You see it in three places:

- The **Portfolio / Cash** chip in the top bar. **Cash** is the pUSD free to trade or withdraw. **Portfolio** is cash plus the live value of your open positions.
- The **Balance** card on the Portfolio page, with **Available**, **In position**, and **Total traded**.
- The **Wallet** modal's **Available** line.

See [Portfolio overview](/portfolio/overview) for how each figure is calculated.

## Profile

Open the user menu (your avatar, top right) and choose **Profile**. The page shows:

- **Header** — your generated avatar, name, email, and "Member since" date.
- **Account** — click **Edit** to change your **Name** or **Phone**, then **Save**. Your email is your login identity and cannot be edited here.
- **Wallet** — your pUSD **Balance** and your two Polygon addresses.
- **Sign out**

## User menu

The avatar menu also offers:

| Item | What it does |
|------|-------------|
| **Profile** | Opens your Profile page. |
| **Referral** | Opens your referral link page. |
| **Light mode** / **Dark mode** | Switches the app theme. Dark is the default. |
| **Log out** | Signs you out. |
