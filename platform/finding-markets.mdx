---
title: "Finding markets"
description: "How to discover what to trade across the Live and Trade hubs."
---

updown has no search box. Markets are discovered by browsing the two hubs, and every market page links to its sibling cadences and its past windows.

## On the Live hub

1. Click **Live** in the top bar.
2. Filter with **All / Sports / Stocks / Crypto** in the left rail (chips on a phone).
3. Step through the **featured panel** with its **‹ ›** pager, or scroll the cards.
4. Click a card, or its **Up** / **Down** button to open the market with that side preselected.

Listings change with the clock: stock cards appear only on weekdays between 4:00 AM and 8:00 PM ET, and sports cards appear and disappear with games. The hub refreshes its listings every minute. See [Live markets](/trading/live-markets).

## On the Trade hub

1. Click **Trade** in the top bar.
2. Pick **All** for one card per subject, or an expiry (**Daily / Weekly / Monthly**) to see that window's prices.
3. Browse the **Outcomes**, **Stocks**, and **Crypto** rows.
4. Click a card or its **Up** / **Down** button.

See [Trade markets](/trading/trade-markets).

## On a market page

- **Switch cadence.** The page title contains an expiry menu (tooltip "Change expiry") listing **15 Minutes**, **End-of-Day**, **End-of-Week**, and **End-of-Month** for that subject, where they exist.
- **Look back.** The **Past** dropdown lists up to eight recently settled windows, for example "2:15 PM ET · Today", each opening that window's result page. Chips beside it jump to the last four.
- **Read the form.** Cards on the Live hub show a **Last 6** strip of recent window results.

## New listings

Sports markets are listed automatically while a two-team game is in play and its odds are between 5% and 95%. Stock, crypto, and outcome subjects are added by the updown team.

<Warning>
**Ctrl/⌘ + K** opens a legacy search from a discontinued product. It searches third-party prediction markets, not updown's Live or Trade markets, and leads to an archived page. Use the hubs instead.
</Warning>
