The **Weekly Trading Competition** ranks traders by realized profit and loss on updown's window markets, with prizes paid in pUSD. In the final six hours of each round, a banner reminds you where you stand, for example "Weekly competition ends in 2h 14m — you're #4, $3.10 behind #3".

## How the competition works

- Rankings are based on **realized P&L on window markets** for windows that **close inside the week**. Sell-to-close counts. Ties are broken by volume.
- A round runs **Friday 12:00 AM to Thursday 11:59 PM ET**, and a fresh competition starts every week. The page shows **This week** with the date range and a **Resets in** countdown.
- Only windows whose settlement succeeded count; pending settlements are excluded.
- The **Rules & Prizes** button opens the full rules for the current round.

The rules copy reads: "Ranked by realized P&L on interval markets this week — what your settled windows paid out minus what you staked, sell-to-close included. Volume alone doesn't rank you; only windows that close inside the week count."

## Prizes

| Place | Prize |
|-------|-------|
| **1st** | $100 |
| **2nd** | $50 |
| **3rd** | $25 |
| **4th–10th** | $5 each |

Winners are paid in **pUSD straight to their wallet** at the end of each round.

## Rankings

The top three get a podium at the top of the page. Below it, each row shows:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **#** | Rank for the current round. |
| **Trader** | Display name (set on your Profile page) with a generated avatar. |
| **Volume** | Face-value volume traded this round. |
| **P&L** | Realized profit or loss this round, signed and colored. |
| **24h** | Rank change over the last day. |

Your row carries a "You" tag and is pinned below the list if you are outside the top. Standings refresh every 10 seconds. Before anyone has traded, the page reads "No trades yet this week — Be the first on the board."
