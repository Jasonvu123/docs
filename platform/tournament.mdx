The **Tournament** is a fixed two-week competition. Click **Tournament** in the top bar to open it. The header shows the dates, the prize pool, a **Starts in** or **Ends in** countdown, and a **Rules & Prizes** link.

## The current round

| | |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | Thursday, Sep 24, 12:00 AM → Wednesday, Oct 7, 11:59 PM ET |
| **Prize pool** | $1,000 |
| **1st place** | $600 |
| **2nd place** | $300 |
| **3rd place** | $100 |

Prizes are paid out after the tournament ends.

## How you are ranked

Rankings are based on your **realized P&L across the whole tournament**, from both **options and interval markets**: what your settled positions paid out minus what you staked, selling back included. The **Rules & Prizes** dialog has the full rules for the current round.

- **All P&L counts.** Options and interval trades both count toward your standing.
- **Realized P&L only**, from positions that **settle or are sold inside the tournament**. Open positions do not count until they settle.
- **Ties** break on traded volume.

## The board

The top three get a podium once three traders are ranked, with the prize shown beside each trophy. Below it, the list is headed "All traders · ranked by P&L" (or "Final standings · ranked by P&L" once the round is over):

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **#** | Rank |
| **Trader** | Icon and username, linking to the public profile |
| **Volume** | Volume traded in the round |
| **P&L** | Realized P&L in the round |
| **24h** | Rank change over the last day |

Your row carries a **You** tag and is pinned below the top 50 if you are outside it. The board refreshes every 10 seconds. Before anyone has traded it reads "No trades yet. Be the first on the board."

In the final six hours, a banner across the app counts down: "Tournament ends in 2h 14m — you're #4, $3.10 behind #3".

## Rolling leaderboards

The side panel's **Leaderboard** tab (wide screens) shows the same ranking over rolling windows: **24H**, **7D**, **30D**, **ALL**, and **Tournament**, with a **Full board →** link. Your Portfolio header's **Rank** stat is your Tournament position.
