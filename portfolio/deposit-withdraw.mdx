---
title: "Deposits & withdrawals"
description: "Fund your account with USDC on Polygon and withdraw native USDC to any Polygon address."
---

updown runs on a single balance. Deposit **USDC on Polygon** and it converts automatically to **pUSD**, your trading balance. Withdraw native USDC to any Polygon address whenever you like. There are no fees on either side and gas is sponsored.

The **Deposit** and **Withdraw** buttons live on the Balance card of your Portfolio page. The **Portfolio / Cash** chip in the top bar also opens the deposit view.

<Warning>
Send **USDC on the Polygon network only**. The deposit modal warns: "Wrong tokens or networks may result in permanent loss." There is no in-app recovery. If it happens, contact [support](/help/support) with the transaction hash.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Deposit">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Wallet modal">
        Click the **Portfolio / Cash** chip, or **Deposit** on your Portfolio page. The first time, complete the one-time **Allow trading** approval and click **Done**. See [Account setup](/account-setup#allow-trading).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Copy your address">
        Under **Deposit**, the card labelled "USDC on Polygon" shows a QR code and your address. Click **Copy Address**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send at least $2 of USDC on Polygon">
        From your exchange or wallet, send USDC to that address on the **Polygon** network. The minimum is **$2**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Watch it convert">
        The modal tracks progress live: *Deposit detected… → Finalizing… → Converting your deposit to pUSD…* and finishes with "Deposit converted to pUSD". You can keep the modal open or come back later. Your very first deposit also sets up your deposit wallet on-chain, which adds a few seconds.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm your balance">
        Click **Go to Portfolio**. The amount appears in **Available**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
    Sent less than $2? The modal explains: "We received $X, but deposits under $2 can't be converted. Send at least $Y more USDC and it all converts automatically — your funds are safe meanwhile."
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Withdraw">
    Withdrawals send **native USDC on Polygon** from your balance to any address you choose. The modal describes it as "Gas-free, usually instant."

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Withdraw modal">
        Click **Withdraw** on your Portfolio page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the recipient">
        Paste the destination in **Recipient address (Polygon)**. It must be a valid 0x address.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the amount">
        Type an amount in **Amount (USDC)** or click **MAX**. The minimum is **$2**. The modal shows **You receive** as "≈ $X USDC".
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm">
        Click **Withdraw**. The success screen reads "Withdrawal confirmed" (or "Withdrawal submitted" while the transaction mines) with a **View transaction →** link to Polygonscan. Choose **Withdraw again** or **Done**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
    "Withdrawals are irreversible — double-check the address."
    </Warning>

    <Note>
    Money committed to open windows cannot be withdrawn until those windows settle. The modal's **Available** shows your full pUSD balance, so a withdrawal that dips into committed funds is refused with a message like "Amount $X exceeds your available $Y ($Z is committed to open interval positions)."
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Supported assets

| Direction | Asset | Network | Minimum | Fee |
|-----------|-------|---------|---------|-----|
| Deposit | USDC | Polygon | $2 | None |
| Held as | pUSD (converted automatically) | — | — | — |
| Withdraw | Native USDC | Polygon | $2 | None, gas sponsored |

Withdrawals appear in **Portfolio → history** as "Withdrawal to 0x…".

<Info>
Restricted accounts keep the ability to withdraw. See [Support](/help/support#restricted-accounts).
</Info>
