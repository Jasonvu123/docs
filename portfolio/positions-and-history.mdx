title: "Positions and history"
description: "Read your open positions, close them, and follow your activity and settled-window pages."
---

Your open positions and activity live on the **Portfolio** page. Interval market pages add a **Live Positions** panel, and options stock pages add a **Positions** table.

## Open positions

Under **Your positions → Open**, one list shows interval positions first, then options.

### Interval rows

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Title** | Image and market title |
| **Status line** | "▲ 15m · closes in 12m", or "15m · settling" once the window has closed |
| **Detail line** | "3.00 contracts · 51¢ → 53¢ · pays $3.00 if up" (entry price → current price) |
| **Value / P&L** | The current value and "+$X (+Y%)", or "awaiting settle" after the close |
| **Button** | **Trade** opens the market page. **View** once the window is settling. A **Settles at close** chip appears instead on markets that have been retired. |

Interval positions refresh every 10 seconds.

### Options rows

Each shows "SPY above $770" or "SPY below $762", a line "▲ Call · Option · closes in 3h 05m" (or "paying out" / "settling"), "1.00 contracts · $1.23 → $1.50", the value and P&L, and a **Trade** button. You cannot sell an option from Portfolio; the button opens the stock page. See [Positions & settlement](/options/positions-and-settlement).

### Orders

The **Orders** tab is for limit orders, which are not yet available. It reads "No open orders".

## Live Positions on an interval market page

The panel above the outcome row shows your contracts in the current window:

- **Buying power** at the top: your available cash.
- One row per side with a color dot, the side label, the contract count, the value, and a **Sell** button.
- A sub-line with "entry 51¢ · now 54¢" and "pays $5.00 if Up".
- Totals: **Value**, **Unrealized** ("+$0.15 (+5.9%)"), and **Total return** (your realized profit or loss on this market).

When you hold nothing it reads "No position in this window yet."

## Closing early

- **Interval:** tap **Sell** on a side's row in **Live Positions** to sell that side's whole position at the current bid. To sell part of it, use the ticket's **Market → Sell** form. See [Placing interval orders](/interval/placing-orders#selling-back).
- **Options:** tap **Sell $X** in the stock page's **Positions** table. See [Positions & settlement](/options/positions-and-settlement#selling-back).

<Tip>
Selling an interval position early gives you the current bid rather than the $1 settlement value. Holding to the close pays more if your side wins, but selling removes the uncertainty.
</Tip>

## Activity

The activity table lists every fill and settlement, newest first, with filters **All activity / Interval / Options / Spot** and columns **Market / Action / Amount / P&L / Time**.

| Action chip | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Buy** | "3.00 contracts @ 51¢" or, for options, "1.00 contracts @ $1.23" |
| **Sell** | Sold back before settlement, with proceeds and P&L |
| **Won** | "· paid $1 each" (interval) or "· settled in the money" (options) |
| **Lost** | "· settled worthless" (interval) or "· expired worthless" (options) |
| **Settled** | "· nothing owed either way" (an interval window where you held both sides) |
| **Refund** | "· no settlement price, premium refunded" (options only) |

Times are relative ("now", "12m", "3h", "5d"), then a date. Interval history shows up to 200 rows.

<Note>
A window you sold out of completely shows only its **Sell** rows. If you held both sides of a window, the winning side is shown. Withdrawals never appear on public profiles.
</Note>

## Settled-window pages

Click an interval activity row's title, or pick a window from the **Past** dropdown on a market page, to open its result page: the eyebrow "Crypto · 15 min · settled", the **Result** ("Up won" or "Down won"), **Open**, **Close**, and the full chart with the level to beat marked "Target".

A page appears shortly after the close. Before it is recorded, it reads "This window isn't recorded yet — Windows are saved shortly after they close." with a **Back to the live market** link.

<Info>
Accounts from before the pivot may see older position types and a **Spot** activity filter. Those belong to a discontinued product and appear only if you still hold positions in it.
</Info>
