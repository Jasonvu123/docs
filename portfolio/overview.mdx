Click **Portfolio** in the top bar. The page is built around two cards, **Balance** and **Portfolio value**, with your positions and history below.

## The Portfolio / Cash chip

The chip in the top bar shows two numbers at all times:

- **Cash** — pUSD available to trade or withdraw.
- **Portfolio** — cash plus the live value of your open positions.

Its tooltip reads "Portfolio = cash + open positions · Cash = available to trade". Click the chip to open the **Wallet** modal.

## Balance card

The headline figure is your total **Balance**. Below it:

| Cell | What it means |
|------|--------------|
| **Available** | pUSD you can spend or withdraw now: your balance minus the cost of contracts still held in open windows. |
| **In position** | The live value of everything you hold in open windows, marked at the current midpoint. |
| **Total traded** | Face-value volume: **$1 per contract** across every buy and sell. It is not the money you have spent. |

The **Deposit** and **Withdraw** buttons sit on this card. See [Deposits & withdrawals](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw).

### What moves Available

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Buy | Minus the cost, held as exposure until the window settles. |
| Sell back | Realized profit or loss applied immediately; the cost basis is released. |
| Window settles | Plus $1 per winning contract, minus the held cost, applied once per window. |
| Deposit | Plus the converted amount. |
| Withdraw | Minus the amount. |

<Warning>
Money in open windows is neither spendable nor withdrawable until those windows settle. The market page calls the spendable figure **Buying power**.
</Warning>

## Portfolio value card

The card charts your total value over time:

- **Ranges:** 1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y, ALL. The default is **1W**.
- **Change line:** the signed change and percentage over the range, for example "+$12.40 (+3.1%) Past week".
- **Hover or drag** the chart and the headline value follows your cursor.

Snapshots are taken every 15 minutes. While the page is open, balances refresh every 15 seconds, positions every 10 seconds, and history and the chart every 30 to 60 seconds.

## P&L

- **Unrealized** is shown per open position: current value minus cost, with a percentage. It reads "awaiting settle" once the window has closed.
- **Realized** is shown per history row and, on each market page, as **Total return** for that market.

Gains are green and losses are red.

## Positions and history

Below the cards are three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|--------------|
| **positions** | Your open positions under **Interval markets**. |
| **Open orders** | Empty until limit orders ship: "Interval trades fill instantly, so nothing rests here yet." |
| **history** | Every buy, sell, and settlement, with **All / Interval** filters. |

See [Positions and history](/portfolio/positions-and-history) for the row-by-row detail.

<Info>
Older accounts may also see **Options**, **Written (covered)**, and **Markets** sections and extra history filters. Those belong to a discontinued product and only appear if you still hold positions in it.
</Info>
