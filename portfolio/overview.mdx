Click **Portfolio** in the top bar, or your avatar → **Profile**. The page is your balance sheet and your public profile in one.

## Cash and Portfolio in the top bar

Two figures are always visible when you are signed in:

- **Cash** — the USDG in your wallet. Tooltip "Cash = available to trade · click to deposit". Click it to open the deposit dialog.
- **Portfolio** — cash plus the live value of your open positions. Tooltip "Portfolio = cash + open positions". Click it to open this page.

Both refresh every 30 seconds.

## The page, top to bottom

1. **Header** — your icon, name, X link, and "@username"; stats **Volume**, **Realized P&L**, and **Rank** (your Tournament position, or "—"); **Following** and **Followers** counts; **Edit profile**, the wallet icon, and the sign-out icon; and a meta line with your trade count, "Joined Sep 2026", and your email (visible only to you).
2. **Value card** — your total value, a change line such as "+$12.40 (+3.1%) 7d", range pills **1D / 1W / 1M / 1Y / ALL** (default 1W), then **Available cash** with "$X in positions" beneath, and the **Withdraw** and **Deposit** buttons. Hover the chart for a point-in-time value. With too little data it reads "Not enough history to chart yet".
3. **Your positions** — a segmented control **Open** (count) / **Orders** (count). See [Positions and history](/portfolio/positions-and-history).
4. **Your top take**, if you have posted one.
5. **Activity** — the trade history table.

## The balance model

| Figure | Where | What it is |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Cash** | Top bar | USDG in your wallet |
| **Portfolio** | Top bar | Cash + open interval positions + open options, both at the current bid |
| **Available cash** | Value card | Cash minus the cost reserved by open interval positions, including windows that have closed but not yet settled |
| **$X in positions** | Value card | Open interval positions at the midpoint (at cost while settling) + open options at the bid (at cost when unquoted) |
| **Total** | Value card headline | Available cash + in positions |
| **Buying power** | Interval market page | Available cash |
| **Wallet** | Options order panel | Cash |
| **Volume** | Header | Interval: $1 per contract over every buy and sell. Options: contracts × the stock price at purchase, counted again on a sale. |
| **Realized P&L** | Header | Interval and options combined |

### How each product moves your cash

- **Interval buys move no money when placed.** The cost is reserved from your available cash. When the window settles, a loss is collected from your wallet and a win is paid in, as one net transfer. Selling back mid-window settles the realized difference immediately.
- **Option buys take the premium from your wallet immediately** and reserve nothing. Sales and settlement payouts arrive in the wallet.

<Warning>
Cash reserved by open interval windows cannot be spent on new interval trades or withdrawn until those windows settle. The top-bar **Cash** figure shows your full wallet, so it can be higher than **Available cash**.
</Warning>

## Your public profile

Anyone can see your page at `/u/your-username`, minus your email and your options positions, and subject to your **Visibility** settings for Wallet, Positions, and Activity. See [Account & profile](/account-and-profile#your-profile) and [Profiles & social](/platform/profiles-and-social).
