title: "Browsing interval markets"
description: "The Interval hub, the Stocks and Crypto pages, market cards, and the market page."
---

Click **Interval** in the top bar to open the hub, headed **Browse markets**. Stocks and crypto also have their own pages, and Home features a rotating selection. For the mechanic behind every window, see [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work).

## The Interval hub

The hub lists every open market as a compact card, with a sub-line such as "All Categories · All Intervals · 45 markets".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Two dropdowns: **category** (**All Categories**, **Stocks**, **Crypto**, with counts) and **interval** (**All Intervals**, **5 min**, **15 min**, **60 min**). Filters are kept in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade from a card, or open it">
    Tap a card's **Up 51¢** or **Down 49¢** button to open the trade dialog with that side selected, or tap the card body to open the market page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or place several at once">
    Turn on **Multi-buy** to pick up to 20 outcomes and place them in one order. See [Multi-buy](/interval/multi-buy).
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Compact cards** show the image and title, **To beat** and **Now** with the move ("▲ $1.20"), the **Up** / **Down** price buttons, and a footer with the cadence and countdown.

Empty states read "No markets right now", "No stock markets right now — Stock markets list while their price feed is live.", or "New recurring markets roll out here as their metrics go live.", with a **Browse all markets** button.

## Stocks and Crypto pages

Home's **Stocks** and **Crypto** sections each have a **See all** link to a category page. The page has:

- An interval tab row **5 min / 15 min / 60 min** with counts. The default is **15 min**.
- A **Multi-buy** toggle.
- The **featured panel**.
- A two-column grid of **live cards**.

### The featured panel

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Title** | The market and cadence, "Bitcoin (BTC) · 15 min" |
| **Market / Pays out / Odds** | Each side with its payout multiple and price, "Up · 1.96x · 51¢" |
| **Closes in** / **Opens in** | Countdown to the close |
| **To beat** and **Now** | The opening price (with the close time) and the live price (with the move) |
| **Chart** | The whole current window so far, with a **Target** pill at the level to beat and avatars of traders who bought, where they bought |

On Home the panel rotates automatically every 9 seconds between options and interval markets, with dots to jump.

<Note>
The "Pays out" multiple is 1 divided by the price. It ignores the spread and fee you pay when you actually fill.
</Note>

### Live cards

Each card shows the image and title, the cadence, the countdown, **To beat** (with the close time), **Now** (with the move), a row per side with its price button and a bar, a **Live** or **Upcoming** tag, and a **Last 6** strip of triangles showing recent window results. Hover a triangle for "2:00 PM window · $325.40 → $325.62 · Up". On Home, cards also carry **Also** links to the subject's other cadences.

## The market page

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Header** | Image, title with the cadence menu ("Apple (AAPL) · 15 Minutes ▾ · Up or Down"), the window line, and the **LIVE** tag |
| **To beat / Now / Time left** | The window's key numbers. See [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work#reading-the-window-header) |
| **Chart** | Buttons **Live / 5m / 15m / 1H / MKT**. **Live** is a two-minute tape and the default; **MKT** is the whole window so far. Buyer avatars are pinned where they bought |
| **Past** | A dropdown of up to eight settled windows ("2:15 PM ET · Today"), plus chips for the last four. Each opens that window's result page |
| **Live Positions** | Your contracts in the current window, with **Sell** buttons and your **Buying power** |
| **Outcome row** | "Up or Down · BTC Price: $77,271.11 · 51%" with **Up 51¢ / Down 49¢** and the ⓘ tie note |
| **Settlement rules** | The exact rules for this market |
| **Similar markets** | Up to three related markets |
| **Ticket** | The right rail on desktop; docked **Buy Up / Buy Down** pills on a phone. See [Placing interval orders](/interval/placing-orders) |

The cadence menu (tooltip "Change expiry") switches between **5 Minutes**, **15 Minutes**, and **60 Minutes** for the same subject.

Stock prices update every second and crypto prices on every trade. The hub's market list refreshes every minute, and stock cards leave the hub while their market is closed. See [market hours](/interval/how-windows-work#market-hours).
