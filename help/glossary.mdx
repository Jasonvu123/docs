---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Key terms used across updown."
---

## A

**Allow trading**
The one-time approval button in the Wallet modal that lets updown convert deposits and place trades without a signature for every action.

**Available**
pUSD you can spend or withdraw right now: your balance minus what is committed to open windows. In the Wallet and Withdraw modals, the Available line shows your full pUSD balance instead.

## B

**Balance**
The headline figure on the Portfolio page: Available plus In position.

**Buying power**
The spendable balance shown in the market page's Live Positions panel. Same idea as Available.

## C

**Cash**
The free-balance cell in the Portfolio / Cash chip: pUSD not tied up in open positions.

**Contract**
The unit you trade. One Up or Down claim on a window, priced 1¢–99¢, paying $1.00 if its side wins and $0 if not. The order book's "Shares" column counts contracts.

## D

**Daily / Weekly / Monthly**
The Trade hub's expiries. One window per UTC day (00:00 – 23:59), per week (Monday 00:00 UTC), or per month (the 1st, 00:00 UTC).

**Deposit wallet (Polygon)**
The Polygon address that holds your pUSD balance. Shown on your Profile page.

**Down**
The side that wins if the market's value closes the window at or below the level to beat. Ties go to Down. On team markets it is named after the second team.

## E

**End-of-Day / End-of-Week / End-of-Month**
The market page's cadence menu names for a subject's Daily, Weekly, and Monthly markets.

**Entry**
Your average purchase price for a side, in cents.

**Expiry**
On the Trade hub, the window length you are trading. Cards show it as "Expires today" or "Expires Sep 30 UTC".

## I

**In position**
The live value of everything you hold in open windows, marked at the current midpoint.

**Interval market**
updown's internal name for any up-or-down window market. It appears in the Portfolio "Interval markets" section and the "Interval" history filter.

## L

**Limit**
A ticket order type that is not yet available. Its button reads "Limit orders open with the order book".

**Live**
The hub of 15-minute markets on stocks, crypto, and in-play sports.

**Live Positions**
The market page panel listing your contracts in the current window, with Sell buttons.

## M

**Market**
One tracked number that runs a series of windows, for example "Apple (AAPL)". Also the ticket order type that spends a dollar amount at the live quote.

**Max payout**
What a Market order returns if its side wins: dollars divided by price.

## N

**Now**
The market's live value.

## O

**Odds**
The chosen side's price read as a chance, for example "51% chance".

**Order book**
updown's resting Up and Down quote: Asks above, Bids below, with "Last" and "Spread" between them. Columns Price, Shares, and Total.

## P

**Past**
The market page dropdown of recently settled windows, each linking to its result page.

**Pays**
The dollars you receive if your side wins, equal to the contracts you hold, for example "pays $5.00 if Up".

**P&L (profit and loss)**
Your gain or loss. Unrealized while a position is open, realized once you sell back or the window settles.

**Polygon**
The blockchain your wallet lives on. Deposits and withdrawals are USDC on Polygon.

**Portfolio**
Cash plus the live value of open positions. Shown in the top-bar chip and on the Portfolio page.

**pUSD**
Your trading balance. Created automatically from USDC deposits and turned back into native USDC on withdrawal.

## Q

**Quick Buy**
Hold-to-buy boxes for $1, $5, and $10. Press and hold for about 0.7 seconds to fill at the live quote.

## R

**Realized**
Profit or loss locked in by selling back or by settlement, for example "+$0.40 realized".

## S

**Settlement**
The automatic close-out of a window: winning contracts are paid $1.00 each and the net amount moves on-chain. History rows read Won, Lost, or Settled.

**Settling**
A position whose window has closed but has not been paid yet, usually for under 5 minutes.

**Signing wallet (Polygon)**
Your Privy embedded wallet. This is the address you send USDC to. Shown on your Profile page and in the Wallet modal.

**Spread**
The gap between updown's best ask and best bid, at least 2¢. It is the only cost of trading.

## T

**Time left**
Countdown to the window close. It turns amber, then red, as the window runs out.

**To beat**
The market's value when the window opened. Up needs a close strictly above it.

**Total return**
Your realized P&L on one market, in dollars and as a percentage of what you staked. Shown in Live Positions.

**Total traded**
Face-value volume: $1 per contract across every buy and sell.

**Trade**
The hub of daily, weekly, and monthly markets on outcomes, stocks, and crypto.

**Trade executed**
The confirmation card shown after a fill.

## U

**Unrealized**
Current value minus cost of the contracts you still hold.

**Up**
The side that wins if the market's value closes the window strictly above the level to beat. On team markets it is named after the tracked team.

**USDC**
The stablecoin you deposit and withdraw, as native USDC on Polygon.

## W

**Window**
One round of a market with a fixed open and close: 15 minutes on Live, a day, week, or month on Trade.

**Won / Lost**
History labels for settled positions.
