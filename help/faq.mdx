## Trading

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What am I actually betting on?">
    Whether a live number closes a window above where it opened. The number can be a stock or crypto price, a team's live win odds, or a prediction-market outcome's chance. You buy **Up** or **Down** contracts, and each winning contract pays $1.00. See [How windows work](/trading/how-windows-work).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the most I can lose?">
    What you paid for your contracts. There is no leverage, shorting, or margin. Every position is fully collateralized when you enter it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if the price finishes exactly where it started?">
    It settles **Down**. As the ticket puts it: "If the price is unchanged, it settles as Down. Up needs a real move."
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a trading fee?">
    No. There is no trading fee today. The only cost is the spread between updown's bid and ask, which is at least 2¢ and widens near 50/50 as a window runs out of time. The "win" and **Max payout** figures in the ticket already include it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is on the other side of my trade?">
    updown's treasury quotes both sides of every window. It caps its exposure at roughly 20 contracts per side per window, so large orders can fill partially.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was my order rejected with &quot;Price moved to …&quot;?">
    The quote changed between what you saw and the fill. updown refuses fills that are more than about 30% plus 3¢ worse than the price shown. Try again at the new price.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I place a limit order?">
    Not yet. The Limit form is visible, but its button reads "Limit orders open with the order book".
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I sell before the window closes?">
    Yes. Hold **Sell** on your row in **Live Positions**, or use **Market → Sell** in the ticket. The realized difference is paid or charged immediately. See [Placing orders](/trading/placing-orders#selling-back).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Live and Trade?">
    **Live** runs 15-minute windows on stocks, crypto, and in-play sports. **Trade** runs one window per day, week, or month on stocks, crypto, and prediction-market outcomes. The ticket and the rules are the same.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I trade Apple right now?">
    Stock markets run only on weekdays from 4:00 AM to 8:00 PM ET. Outside those hours the page reads "Market closed — stock markets trade weekdays 4:00 AM – 8:00 PM ET." Crypto markets stay open 24/7.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The game ended in the middle of a window. What happens?">
    The odds freeze and trading pauses ("Trading paused — game over"). The window settles at the frozen odds and payouts follow shortly. No new window opens after the final one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Settlement and balances

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When do I get paid?">
    Automatically. A settlement pass runs every 5 minutes, and the page triggers one when a window rolls. Your history shows **Won** or **Lost** and your **Cash** updates. Until then the position reads "settling" or "awaiting settle". There is nothing to claim.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between Portfolio and Cash?">
    **Cash** is pUSD not tied up in open windows. **Portfolio** adds the live value of your open positions. See [Portfolio overview](/portfolio/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does my position show a different value on the market page and on Portfolio?">
    The market page marks at what updown would pay you right now (its bid). Portfolio marks at the midpoint. The two converge when you sell or the window settles.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is Total traded bigger than what I've spent?">
    **Total traded** is face-value volume: $1 per contract across every buy and sell, not the dollars you spent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Deposits and withdrawals

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I deposit?">
    Open the **Wallet** modal from the **Portfolio / Cash** chip, click **Allow trading** once, then send at least $2 of USDC on Polygon to the address shown. It converts to pUSD automatically. Keep the page open or come back later. See [Deposits & withdrawals](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I sent less than $2. Is it lost?">
    No. The modal explains that deposits under $2 can't be converted and tells you how much more to send. Once the total reaches $2 it all converts, and your funds are safe in the meantime.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I sent the wrong token or network.">
    The deposit modal warns that "Wrong tokens or networks may result in permanent loss." There is no in-app recovery. Contact [support](/help/support) with the transaction hash.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I withdraw, and is there a fee?">
    Click **Withdraw** on your Portfolio page, paste any Polygon address, and enter an amount of at least $2. You receive native USDC on Polygon, gas is sponsored, and it usually arrives instantly. Money in open windows can't be withdrawn until those windows settle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a crypto wallet to sign up?">
    No. updown uses embedded wallets powered by Privy. Sign in with your email or Google account and a wallet is created for you. You never install a browser extension or manage a seed phrase.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and eligibility

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is updown available in my country?">
    Trading is not available in every region. If you are in a restricted region, a globe icon appears in the top bar with the tooltip "Trading is unavailable in your region". If you are unsure about your eligibility, contact [support](/help/support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My account says &quot;Account flagged&quot;. What now?">
    Trading, deposits, and rewards are restricted, but as the notice says, "Your balance is not affected — you can still withdraw at any time." If you believe it is a mistake, contact support on Discord or by email. See [Support](/help/support#restricted-accounts).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Click **Contact us** in the bottom bar to send a message from the app, or use email, X, or Discord. See [Support](/help/support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
