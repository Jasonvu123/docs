## Sending a message from the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Click **Contact us** in the bottom bar. The page is headed **Contact Us** and lists the channels above, followed by a **Send a Message** form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your details">
    **Username** is filled from your account and cannot be edited. **Email** is prefilled and can be changed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Type up to **5,000 characters** in **Message**. A counter shows your usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Message**. The button reads "Sending…" and then "Message Sent!".
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The form needs a signed-in account, because **Username** is required and filled from your profile. If you cannot sign in, use email or Discord instead.
</Note>

## What to include

- The **transaction hash** for any deposit or withdrawal question. Deposits and withdrawals are on Polygon and can be checked on Polygonscan.
- The **market and window time** for any settlement question. Every settled window has its own page reachable from the **Past** dropdown or your history.

## Mis-sent deposits

Deposit **USDC on the Polygon network only**. The deposit modal warns: "Wrong tokens or networks may result in permanent loss." There is no automated recovery. Contact support with the transaction hash as soon as possible.

## Restricted accounts

If updown flags an account for suspicious activity, an **Account flagged** notice appears:

> This account has been flagged for suspicious activity and is restricted from trading, deposits, and rewards. Your balance is not affected — you can still withdraw at any time. If you believe this is a mistake, contact us on Discord or at hello@thetalabs.gg and we'll take a look.

Withdrawals keep working for restricted accounts.

## Service status

The bottom bar shows an **Online** / **Offline** dot that reflects whether the app can reach its servers. There is no separate status page.
